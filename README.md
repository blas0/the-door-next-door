# The Door Next Door

Finds your way through the Barrows tunnels: outlines every door that's unlocked
for you, and drops a door's outline once you've been through it, so the doors
still lit are the ones you haven't tried.

## Doors

- Outlines both halves of each unlocked tunnel door (setting: Door color).
  Locked doors get no outline.
- A door's outline goes away once you go through it: when the door you clicked
  Open on swings open, or when you step across it. It stays off for the rest
  of the trip, even after the door shuts behind you.
- Every door lights up again when you're back above ground or loot the chest.

Each player gets their own door layout, so what you see is your own, and other
players opening doors in the tunnels don't change it.

## What it doesn't do

The Door Next Door only draws on screen. It doesn't click, add or change menu
options, change click areas, or plan a route for you.

Barrows facts are from the [OSRS Wiki](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Barrows).
